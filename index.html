About Opus Studio

Where musicianship
meets artistry.

At Opus Studio, we believe music education is more than learning notes and technique. It is about developing confidence, discipline, creativity, and a lifelong connection with music.

Our approach combines a strong technical foundation with thoughtful, individualized instruction. Every student learns differently, and we believe their musical education should reflect their unique goals, strengths, and personality.

Whether a student is beginning their musical journey, preparing for a recital, audition, or competition, or pursuing music at an advanced level, Opus Studio provides the guidance and support needed to grow with confidence.

Our goal is not simply to teach students how to play an instrument, but to help them become thoughtful, expressive, and independent musicians.

Excellence in music. Artistry for life.

Meet Your Teacher

❧

Pin-Hsuan (Penny)

Cello · Founder

Penny earned a Master of Music from Mannes School of Music under the tutelage of Ronald Thomas, and completed her Doctor of Musical Arts degree at Boston University under Professor Michael Reynolds.

Born in Kaohsiung, she began her musical journey at the age of nine with Zhixian Wu, and went on to study with Ya-Ru Lian, Ouyang Lingyi, and Yu-Wen Chou, receiving further guidance from Rhonda Rider, Julia Lichten, Lluis Claret, Pei-Sian Ng, Wen-Sinn Yang, Suren Bagratuni, and others.

She enjoys a distinguished career as a soloist, chamber musician, and educator. She toured with the Asian Youth Orchestra alongside artists including Steven Isserlis, appeared in the Ruby Yang film In Search of Perfect Consonance, and held full-scholarship fellowships at the Tanggong and Bowdoin music festivals.

Penny has served as principal cellist of the Regina Opera, Taipei Philharmonic Youth Orchestra, and Lanyang Symphony Orchestra, joined the fellowship program at Montclair Orchestra, and in summer 2025 was invited to the cello faculty of the Lanyang Summer Music Festival. She has given recitals in Taipei, New York, Boston, Shanghai, Tianjin, Hong Kong, Tokyo, and Osaka.

D.M.A. Boston University M.M. Mannes School of Music Asian Youth Orchestra Principal Cello · Regina Opera
